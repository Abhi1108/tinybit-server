Delete Your TinyBit Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your TinyBit account and what happens to your data.

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion directly inside the TinyBit app:

  1. Open the TinyBit app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
No longer have the app installed, or can't log in?
Email support@tinybit.cloud from the phone number or email address linked to your account and request account deletion. We will verify your identity and process the request manually.

What happens after you request deletion

  • Your account is deactivated immediately — you're logged out on all devices and can no longer sign in.
  • All push notification tokens linked to your account are cleared right away.
  • Your account and data enter a 30-day grace period before permanent deletion. This window exists to prevent accidental or unauthorized deletion requests.
  • To cancel a deletion request during the 30-day window, contact support@tinybit.cloud — this cannot currently be undone from within the app.
  • After 30 days, your data is permanently and irreversibly purged, except where noted below.

What data is kept, and why

  • Other people's data isn't affected. If you're a guardian, deleting your account does not delete the elder profiles or records that belong to the elders you manage — that data belongs to them, not you. Likewise, if an elder deletes their account, their guardians' own accounts and data are unaffected. Only the link between your account and theirs, and messages exchanged between you, are removed.
  • A minimal internal record that your account was deleted (an internal ID and the date) is kept in our operational audit log for security and abuse-prevention purposes. This record does not contain your name, email, phone number, or any of your app data.

For more detail on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions about this page can be sent to support@tinybit.cloud.
